2. Camera, Photos & Document Image Data Handling

AP Exam App incorporates the Magic Scanner™ and FRQ Grader™ to assist students with difficult math, science, history, and literature problems. Below is an exhaustive disclosure of how camera and photo data is gathered, processed, and safeguarded:

A. Purpose of Camera & Photo Access

  • Camera Access (`android.permission.CAMERA` / iOS `NSCameraUsageDescription`): Requested solely when you actively open the Magic Scanner to capture a real-time photograph of a physical homework assignment, textbook equation, graph, or handwritten AP Free-Response Question (FRQ) answer sheet.
  • Photo Gallery Access (`android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / iOS `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`): Requested solely when you tap the "Upload from Gallery" button to select an existing photo or screenshot of a study problem from your device.

B. On-Device Compression & Memory Processing

  • Client-Side Optimization: Before leaving your phone, images are compressed on-device (resized to max 1200px and converted to optimized JPEG format) to minimize data consumption and prevent unnecessary network footprint.
  • In-Memory Volatile Processing: When an image is uploaded for analysis, our server receives it directly into a transient memory buffer (RAM via `multer.memoryStorage()`). Images are NEVER written to a permanent disk, public web directory, or persistent server database.
  • Immediate Buffer Purging: Once the optical character recognition (OCR) and pedagogical explanation are generated and streamed back to your screen, the in-memory image buffer is immediately flushed and garbage-collected. We do not maintain any long-term repository of student photographs.

C. Prohibition of Facial Recognition & Biometric Surveillance

Strict Non-Biometric Guarantee: The Application uses camera inputs strictly for document OCR and academic problem transcription. We do NOT perform facial recognition, facial landmark detection, emotional profiling, iris scanning, or biometric identification. If a student's photo inadvertently contains a human face, our algorithms ignore facial features and extract only academic text and symbols.

3. AI Tutoring & Multimodal Inference Transparency

The Application leverages advanced enterprise multimodal artificial intelligence models, powered by Google Gemini AI APIs, to deliver step-by-step problem explanations, AP rubric grading, formula breakdowns, and the AP Trap Radar™ distractor diagnostic.

  • Zero Model Training Guarantee: Under our direct Enterprise API agreements with Google Cloud, your prompts, typed questions, uploaded homework images, handwriting scans, and chat conversations are NEVER used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve Google's public foundation models or any proprietary third-party AI models.
  • Stateless Academic Inference: AI inference sessions are stateless. Data sent to the endpoint is evaluated solely to deliver immediate pedagogical guidance and is not cataloged for cross-user profiling.
  • Voice Tutor Speech Processing: When using the AI Voice Tutor, voice input is processed into text tokens transiently and immediately discarded. We do not generate, archive, or analyze voiceprints or biometric audio patterns.

4. 1v1 Multiplayer Quiz Battle Privacy

AP Exam App features a real-time multiplayer arena where students test their knowledge in 5-round rapid-fire AP quiz battles:

  • Pseudonymous Public Profiles: Multiplayer battles display only your chosen nickname or display name (e.g., "Student", "Alex"), your avatar symbol, and your selected AP grade level (e.g., "11th Grade • AP Scholar").
  • No Geolocation Tracking: We do NOT collect, track, or share GPS coordinates, physical locations, or IP-derived street addresses during matchmaking.
  • No In-Game Chat or Private Messaging: To guarantee minor safety and eliminate cyberbullying, the 1v1 battle feature has NO open text chat, direct messaging, or private communications between opponents. Interaction is strictly limited to synchronized multiple-choice quiz questions.
  • Anonymous / Guest Play: Students can participate in 1v1 Quick Matches in Guest Mode without ever linking an email address or creating an account.
  • Ephemeral Matchmaking Rooms: Matchmaking tickets and game rooms are stored in transient server memory and are automatically expunged after battle completion or 25 seconds of inactivity.

6. Device Storage & Permissions

The Application requests access to select hardware capabilities strictly to fulfill core study functions:

  • Local Document Storage & PDF Generation (`@capacitor/filesystem`): The app generates offline AP formula sheets, cram cheat sheets, and practice exam PDFs. These are saved directly to your device's local Downloads or Documents directory so you can print them or study without an internet connection.
  • Haptics & Vibration (`@capacitor/haptics`): Provides gentle tactile feedback when you lock in an answer or answer correctly during quiz battles. No data is collected or transmitted.
  • Network State (`@capacitor/network`): Monitors whether your device is connected to Wi-Fi, cellular, or offline, allowing the app to smoothly switch to offline study mode.

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request a complete disclosure of the specific categories and pieces of personal information collected about you over the preceding 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and personal study records.
  • Right to Correct: You have the right to rectify inaccurate personal details within your profile.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: We do NOT sell your personal data or share your information for cross-context behavioral advertising; therefore, no opt-out request is necessary.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, alter pricing, or degrade features if you choose to exercise your privacy rights.

12. Data Security & Encryption Protocol

We maintain rigorous physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards to protect student data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • In-Transit Encryption: All communications between your device and our servers use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with strong cipher suites.
  • At-Rest Encryption: Cloud databases (Firestore) and authentication records are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Granular Security Rules: Database read and write rules require cryptographic authentication tokens, ensuring students can strictly access only their own individual study records.
